01 — ABOUT THE CLASS

Coding should feel like creating.

NAJAZA Coding Class introduces children to programming through Scratch — a visual coding platform where they can build animations, games and interactive stories.

Instead of simply watching a teacher code, students are encouraged to experiment, make decisions, fix mistakes and create something of their own.

🎮 Game design 🧠 Problem solving ✨ Creativity 💻 Coding basics
02 — WHAT THEY'LL LEARN

From first block
to final project.

Every lesson builds on the last, giving students the confidence to turn a blank Scratch project into a working game.

⚡

Scratch Basics

Understand sprites, the stage, code blocks and how Scratch projects work.

🏃

Motion & Animation

Bring characters to life using movement, looks, sounds and events.

🎮

Build Games

Use conditions and interactions to create a playable game.

🏆

Score & Timers

Add points, countdowns, winning conditions and game-over screens.

📡

Broadcasts

Make different parts of a project communicate and create game levels.

🚀

Final Project

Plan and build an original project using the skills learned throughout the camp.

03 — THE PROGRAMME

9 lessons. One big creation.

A structured three-week journey from beginner concepts to an independent final project.

WEEK 01
Lesson 1 Meet Scratch • Imagine, Code, Create, Change
Lesson 2 Bring Your Characters to Life • Motion, Looks, Sounds & Events
Lesson 3 Build Your First Game • Touch detection, score & win messages
WEEK 02
Lesson 4 Keep Score • Catch The Star game
Lesson 5 Timers & Winning • Countdown and game-over screens
Lesson 6 Broadcasts & Levels • Start, win and game-over screens
WEEK 03
Lesson 7 Plan & Build • Project inspiration and planning
Lesson 8 Build & Improve • Develop the final project
Lesson 9 Showcase • Finish, present and celebrate
